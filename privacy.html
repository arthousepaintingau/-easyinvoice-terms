Privacy Policy — EasyInvoice AU

Effective Date: 20 August 2026

Art House Painting Pty Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") operates the EasyInvoice AU mobile application ("EasyInvoice" or "the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently.

This Privacy Policy explains what information EasyInvoice handles, how that information is stored and used, the role of third-party services, and how you can make privacy-related requests or complaints.

1. Information We Collect and Hold

EasyInvoice is designed with a local-first approach. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to us in order to use its core invoicing and quoting features.

Information You Enter Into the App

Depending on how you use EasyInvoice, you may enter information such as:

  • Business name and business contact details;
  • Australian Business Number (ABN) and other business information;
  • Customer or client names and contact details;
  • Invoice and quote information;
  • Product or service descriptions;
  • Prices, amounts, tax information and payment details entered into invoices;
  • Other information you choose to include in your business documents.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is used by the App to provide its invoicing, quoting, document generation and related features.

2. Local Storage

EasyInvoice is designed so that your invoice, quote and business information is stored locally on your device rather than being uploaded to our own servers for cloud storage.

We do not operate a cloud database for your invoice or customer records. Accordingly, we generally do not have access to the business records that you create and store locally within the App.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of important business records. Deleting the App, losing your device, or losing access to your device may result in loss of locally stored information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information entered into EasyInvoice is used to provide the functionality of the App, including:

  • Creating and managing quotes and invoices;
  • Generating and exporting business documents;
  • Calculating amounts and applicable tax information based on information entered by you;
  • Saving and retrieving your locally stored business records;
  • Providing subscription-related functionality.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

4. Apple App Store and Subscriptions

EasyInvoice may offer optional paid subscription features through Apple's App Store.

Purchases and subscription transactions are processed through Apple's App Store and associated Apple services. We do not receive or store your credit card number, bank account details, or other payment card information through the App.

Apple may process transaction and subscription information in accordance with Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

For information about how Apple handles personal information, please refer to Apple's privacy information available through Apple's official website.

5. Third-Party Services

EasyInvoice may rely on Apple services necessary to provide App Store functionality, including subscription and purchase verification.

Apart from services required to provide App Store functionality, EasyInvoice does not intentionally upload your invoice, quote, customer or business records to third-party cloud services.

If additional third-party services are introduced in the future that collect or process personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled by EasyInvoice.

Because your invoice and business records are primarily stored locally on your device, the security of your device, operating system, passcode and associated account credentials also plays an important role in protecting your information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because EasyInvoice primarily stores invoice and business information locally on your device, we generally do not retain copies of those records on our servers.

You can delete locally stored information through the functionality provided by the App or by deleting the App from your device, subject to any device backup or operating-system functionality that may retain a copy.

Subscription and transaction information may be retained and processed by Apple in accordance with Apple's applicable policies and legal obligations.

8. Access and Correction

You may request information about personal information that we hold about you and request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Because most information created within EasyInvoice is stored locally on your device and is not accessible to us, you can generally view and edit that information directly within the App.

For privacy-related requests concerning information held by us, please contact us using the details below.

9. Privacy Requests and Complaints

If you have a question, concern or complaint about how we handle personal information, please contact us first so that we can investigate and respond.

We will handle privacy complaints in accordance with applicable Australian privacy requirements.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have the right to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

10. Overseas Disclosure

EasyInvoice does not intentionally upload your invoice, quote or customer records to overseas cloud services operated by us.

However, Apple and other service providers involved in App Store functionality may process information in Australia or overseas in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

EasyInvoice is a general business productivity application and is not directed specifically to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of providing the App.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to EasyInvoice, applicable laws, or our information-handling practices.

When changes are made, the updated effective date will be displayed at the top of this Privacy Policy.
